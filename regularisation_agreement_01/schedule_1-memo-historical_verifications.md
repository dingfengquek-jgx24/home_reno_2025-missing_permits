# Schedule 1 — Verification steps Roque must perform

This memo lists the verification steps required by Schedule 1. It is a working summary, not an operative part of the agreement.

> **Proposed Schedule 1 change — not yet incorporated:** Roque should not be required to have the written P.E. confirmations by the Effective Date. The confirmations need to be obtained and shared with both Homeowners as part of the complete verification package by the Verification Deadline. The operative Schedule 1 draft has not yet been updated and currently contains an Effective Date requirement.

## 1. Investigate every assurance in one process

Open a single Register covering every assurance and question in Schedule 1, including signing-stage facts, both P.E. documents, the work history, each company’s conduct and later updates. Break out separate findings by person, company, work item, role, document and date where needed. Mark each finding **adopted, corrected or unresolved**, with the evidence, limitations and any further action.

## 2. Verify both P.E. documents and their use

For PE-1 (balcony awning) and PE-2 (staircase railings), confirm:

- The exact document, revision, author or endorsing P.E., authenticity and attribution.
- Which installed works and revisions each document covers, and any difference from what was installed.
- Whether the P.E. assessed the completed installation or only its design or calculations; record inspection, supervision and assessment dates separately from the document date.
- What evidence supported the assessment, its dates and limitations, and whether the documents are suitable for the proposed regularisation now.
- Any required inspection, testing, updated endorsement, rectification or other action. For the railings, obtain the P.E.’s express assessment of the reported corrosion and its implications for present safety and suitability.
- Roque’s rights and authorisations to use the documents, including submissions through Saege, and the P.E.’s position on reusing them without a new visit or test. Record any relevant HDB directions.

The required affirmative written P.E. confirmations must address the actual installed works, present suitability, evidence and limitations, and any requirements. A design-only endorsement, a verbal account or Roque’s summary is insufficient. If the P.E. requires additional site visits, inspections or testing, Schedule 1 does not authorise those steps; they require a separate written agreement or amendment signed by Roque and both Homeowners.

## 3. Establish the factual work and permit chronology

For the staircase railings and balcony awning separately, create the sourced factual chronology that the role, knowledge and conduct assessments in items 4 and 6 will use. Record the dates and evidence for instructions, permit checks, applications and decisions, physical installation, P.E. preparation and endorsement, handover, discovery of missing permits, homeowner notification and HDB correspondence. Distinguish planned dates from actual events and verified dates from unknown dates; reconcile the calendar entries, including the planned P.E. submission, planned railing installation and “GLASS INSTALLATION” entry.

As part of that chronology, identify who instructed, authorised and performed the physical works, including each subcontractor, scope and instructing party. Identify who was responsible for identifying permit requirements, applying, obtaining approval and checking approval before work began, and what instructions were actually given. Record Saege’s account of instructions, including the speaker, date, recipient, relevant wording where available and supporting records; distinguish first-hand records from relayed accounts. Obtain the HDB notice to Saege and its response, recording the exact wording, date, addressee, stated basis, affected works and required action, or document their unavailability and the resulting uncertainty. Reconcile conflicting accounts without treating the notice alone as proof of who performed the work.

## 4. Assess roles, knowledge and disclosure using the item 3 chronology

### 4.1 Company knowledge and disclosure

Using the chronology and evidence assembled under item 3, establish when Roque and Saege learned permits were missing, who knew, why the Homeowners were not told earlier, and what was said at handover. Identify the people involved and supporting evidence. Record company-to-company communications about the missing permits as well as communications with the Homeowners.

### 4.2 Individual roles and conduct

For Regina, Hanyong, Nicholas and Cayden, use the item 3 chronology to verify each person’s full name, company relationship and capacities, relevant periods, actual functions and authority for each work item. Check Regina’s claimed roles separately and verify Hanyong’s proposed account about whether he managed or coordinated the railing or awning works for either company. For each person, assess oversight and permit responsibilities, delegations and follow-up; instructions or authorisations; permit checks; knowledge of permit status; communications and disclosures; and material prior assurances. Do not infer someone’s role or lack of involvement from their title or lack of recollection.

## 5. Verify Saege’s work and current eligibility

Use the performance, subcontractor and instruction records in item 3 to verify whether Saege performed each work directly or through subcontractors, and identify any gaps or inconsistencies in the accounts. Separately check Saege’s current identity, relevant HDB registration, restrictions, suspensions, conditions and outstanding directions. Confirm the authority and required registrations of the proposed applicant, submitting contractor and P.E.; obtain current evidence and recheck before submission.

## 6. Assess each company’s conduct using the item 3 chronology

For Roque and Saege separately, assess the chronology and evidence from item 3 to determine whether any director, employee or representative—including non-PMs—instructed, authorised or agreed to start or continue either work while a required permit was absent. Identify each relevant instruction, authorisation or agreement, the person’s company capacity, its date, recipient, substance and scope, permit status at that time, and supporting records. Identify what installers relied on as authority, who gave it, whether it was conditional on permit approval, and who bypassed any condition. Assess separately what checks were actually made, what each person knew or believed, and any mistaken assumptions.

Also investigate intentional disregard of permit obligations, withholding information from the Homeowners or between the companies, representations that applications or approvals were complete, and permit-related charges or payments where services had already been decided against. Disclose material exceptions and uncertainty. Treat administrative omission, authorisation without checking, knowing authorisation, concealment, false representation and misleading charging as distinct matters.

## 7. Support the findings and document the review

Index supporting and contradictory records against the relevant Register entries. Identify who performed each check or supplied each new account, their roles, involvement and knowledge limits, and the enquiry and review dates. Obtain written confirmation of new material factual accounts where available; record unconfirmed oral accounts and unavailable records accurately. The director must review every entry, assess evidence and sources, pursue further enquiries where accounts are incomplete or inconsistent, and sign Roque’s corporate adoption after that review.

## 8. Deliver and update the complete package

By the agreed Verification Deadline, provide both Homeowners the Register, indexed evidence, required P.E. documents and confirmations, and signed corporate adoption. Nicholas must personally email both Homeowners the actual complete P.E. records, stating his full legal name and that he acts as a Roque director. Continue disclosing material discoveries or changes promptly, update affected Register entries, and complete pre-submission rechecks. Both Homeowners must expressly accept the identified basis in writing before regularisation proceeds. The Verification Deadline is blank in the draft and must be completed before signing.
