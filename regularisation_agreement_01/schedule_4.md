# Schedule 4 — Regularisation Management, Supervision and Coordination

## 1. Scope and responsibility

1.1 This Schedule governs management and performance of regularisation of the staircase railings and balcony awning (the “Specified Works”), using existing records and evidence, including verification enquiries, applications, responses to HDB and technical document review. Additional site visits, inspections, testing and physical works are outside this Agreement and must not be arranged or carried out under it. If any such step is required, Roque must disclose the requirement and must not proceed with the affected regularisation process. Such steps require a separately negotiated written agreement or an express written extension or amendment signed by Roque and both Homeowners, specifying scope, responsibilities, costs, timetable and any changes to the P.E. requirements. Neither party is obliged to agree. Disclosure, discussion, homeowner acceptance of personnel or an execution plan does not authorise these steps or alter existing deadlines or termination provisions. This Schedule does not govern toilet rectification or other unrelated renovation works.

1.2 Roque must arrange and maintain the personnel, authority, coordination and supervision required to carry out this regularisation properly. Roque remains responsible under this Agreement for ensuring compliance by Saege and persons engaged by either company for this arrangement. Allocation or delegation of a function does not relieve Roque of that obligation. Naming Saege, a P.E. or an individual does not itself make that person a contracting party or impose a personal warranty or guarantee.

1.3 This Schedule does not authorise commencement, submission or use of homeowner consent. Schedule 1's verification, disclosure, acceptance, P.E. and cessation requirements and Schedule 2's assistance and payment requirements continue to apply. Technical decisions and certifications must be made by persons competent and authorised for the relevant function; a management appointment does not confer professional authority.

## 2. Personnel and allocation of functions

2.1 Before signing, Roque must complete the personnel record below. Each appointment and allocation must be agreed with the person concerned, with supporting written confirmation supplied to both Homeowners. Record full legal names, company capacities, responsibilities, decision-making authority, reporting lines and arrangements for absence. The personnel record may specify additional persons, including newly appointed personnel, beyond Nicholas, Cayden, Hanyong and other persons identified in Schedule 1. It is not limited to persons involved in the original renovation or historical verification. Each additional person must meet the same identification, appointment-confirmation, authority and responsibility requirements under this Schedule and the applicable suitability-verification requirements under Schedule 1, clause 3.8. Identify Nicholas's and Cayden's roles expressly, and Hanyong's role if proposed; state expressly where a person has no operational role. A title, informal assistance or a general statement that the companies will handle matters is insufficient.

Identify responsibility for each function separately, including the allocation for each Specified Work where it differs. Identify who decides, who performs and who checks each material step; do not leave responsibility dependent on an unconfirmed assumption that another person will act.

2.3 The functions listed in the personnel table are not exhaustive. Roque must add a separate entry for every other function that will be performed in carrying out regularisation within this Agreement's authorised scope. Each added entry must identify the function, the person or persons performing it, their company capacities, responsibilities, decision-making authority, reporting arrangements, appointment confirmation and absence arrangements to the same standard as the listed functions. Roque must complete these entries before signing for functions then contemplated. If an additional function is identified later, Roque must promptly disclose it and propose the additional entry in writing to both Homeowners before it is performed. Any associated change in appointees or material change of responsibilities is subject to clause 4.2, including its approval and pause requirements. ## 3. Management and execution controls

3.1 Before the first regularisation submission, Roque must supply both Homeowners with a written execution plan covering each Specified Work, consistent with the verified basis under Schedule 1. It must identify the intended steps, responsible persons, sequence, target dates, dependencies, required approvals and consents, technical reviews, reporting arrangements and completion evidence. Identify any uncertainty and the person responsible for resolving it. Changes affecting the verified basis, proposed method or agreed responsibilities require the disclosure and acceptance prescribed by Schedule 1.

3.2 The overall manager must coordinate the participants, track outstanding tasks and authority deadlines, follow up delegated tasks and escalate unresolved technical, compliance or responsibility issues to Roque's authorised decision-maker. Roque must ensure that the named persons actually carry out their allocated functions. Homeowner enquiries must be answered by a person with the relevant knowledge and authority, or referred promptly to that person with a stated response date.

3.3 Before each submission, the allocated person must check and record that the applicable verification, acceptance, consent, approval and technical prerequisites have been satisfied. Identify the approval or authority relied upon and applicable conditions. An application, approval, P.E. endorsement or permission to investigate does not extend this Agreement to additional site visits, inspections, testing or physical works.

3.4 Roque must report progress in writing to both Homeowners at least weekly while regularisation is active, and at each material submission, HDB response, technical document-review or completion milestone. Reports must identify completed steps, outstanding issues, responsible persons, next steps and changes to target dates. Material obstacles, unsafe conditions, departures from the agreed method, missed approval conditions and gaps in responsibility must be disclosed promptly and before an affected further step. A report does not replace Schedule 1's required verification, corporate adoption or acceptance.

## 4. Continuity, absence and replacement

4.1 Roque must maintain effective coverage of each required function until completion or termination. If an appointee leaves, becomes unavailable, lacks authority or declines responsibility, Roque must notify both Homeowners promptly, identify the affected functions and propose a suitably competent and authorised replacement or revised allocation with supporting written confirmation.

4.2 Before any change in appointees, including an addition, replacement, removal or temporary substitution, or any material change of responsibilities, Roque must submit a written proposal to both Homeowners stating the reasons for the proposed change, the affected functions, the proposed effective date and the nominee's identity, company capacity, competence, responsibilities and authority, with supporting appointment confirmation where applicable. The proposal requires both Homeowners' express written acceptance before implementation under Schedule 1, clause 3.8. Either Homeowner may reject the proposal by providing a written reason to Roque, copied to the other Homeowner. Rejection does not require Roque's agreement with that reason, and neither Homeowner is obliged to accept an alternative appointee. Silence, receipt of the proposal or continued discussions do not constitute acceptance. Notification of a departure or unavailability does not authorise Roque to implement a replacement or revised allocation without that acceptance. From notification of a departure, unavailability or other event requiring a change, or submission of a change proposal, whichever occurs first, Roque must pause all regularisation activities under this Agreement and ensure that Saege and persons engaged by either company do likewise until both Homeowners have expressly approved the proposed change in writing and the approved arrangements are in place. This pause includes applications, submissions, responses to HDB and further implementation steps, even where a particular step is not directly affected by the proposed change. Rejection, silence or continued discussions do not permit resumption; an alternative proposal must receive both Homeowners' express written approval before regularisation resumes. During the pause, Roque must preserve records, track deadlines, disclose any imminent authority deadline promptly to both Homeowners and comply with applicable cessation duties. The pause does not extend any contractual deadline or suspend payment, disclosure or record-handover obligations. Action required by law or immediately necessary to address a safety risk remains subject to Schedule 1, clause 5.6 and its notification requirements.

4.3 Acceptance of a replacement does not resolve a disputed historical account, waive a breach or release an accrued claim. The Homeowners are not required to assume a vacant management, coordination or supervision function.

## 5. Records, completion and continuing obligations

5.1 Roque must maintain and supply both Homeowners with a consolidated record of the current personnel arrangements, execution plan and revisions, submissions and authorisations used, approvals and conditions, relevant correspondence, technical document reviews, identified deficiencies and submission corrections. Identify document versions, dates and responsible persons. Supply material submission and technical-review records with the relevant milestone report, identifying unavailable records and the steps taken to obtain them.

5.2 At completion, Roque must deliver an indexed final package showing HDB's written position on regularisation of each Specified Work, fulfilment of applicable conditions, relevant P.E. confirmations, completion records within the authorised scope, and all outstanding matters. Distinguish HDB approval, professional certification and contractor confirmation. Receipt or review by the Homeowners is not independent certification of accuracy, workmanship or safety or a release of claims.

5.3 On termination, cessation and record handover are governed by Schedule 1. Roque must also hand over the current personnel record, execution plan, task status and relevant records maintained under this Schedule. Accrued rights and record-handover obligations survive completion or termination. This Schedule creates no additional homeowner assistance duty or fee arrangement and does not alter Schedule 2 or the reservation of Original Contract rights.
