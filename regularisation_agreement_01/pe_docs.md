# P.E. Documents

Roque proposed to use two existing P.E. endorsed documents for regularisation.

The two documents are:

- WIP (for staircase railings)
- WIP (for balcony awning)

Hanyong from Roque has already stated in WhatsApp that these documents are authorised for use by the P.E. for regularisation.

Since P.E. endorsements has and require paper trails, it is reasonable to expect Roque to have obtained the authorisation from the P.E.s who endorsed, and to verify that they are suitable for the regularisation works.

Accordingly, clause 2.5 requires authorisation, present suitability and the required written P.E. confirmations to exist at signing. Nicholas, acting in his capacity as Roque's director and for and on behalf of Roque, must personally send the delivery email to both Homeowners. Roque has seven calendar days after the Effective Date solely to deliver authentic, complete copies of the existing endorsements and confirmations to both Homeowners. This is not additional time to obtain substantive authorisation or suitability confirmation. No regularisation may proceed before delivery and compliance with the other verification-first requirements.

Absence of required confirmation at signing or an adverse P.E. response triggers immediate automatic termination. Failure merely to deliver the existing records by the seven-day deadline triggers cessation and either Homeowner's right to terminate by notice, without automatic termination or, by itself, proof of a false substantive assurance. See Schedule 1, clauses 2.5 and 5.13.
