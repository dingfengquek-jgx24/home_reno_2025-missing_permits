# Regularisation Agreement

## Immediate effect and verification before regularisation

This Agreement, including both Schedules, becomes binding and effective immediately when Roque and both Homeowners have signed it. If signatures are given on different dates, the date of the last signature is the **“Effective Date”**. Delivery or acknowledgement of the verification package is not a condition of the Agreement becoming binding.

As the first step under this arrangement, Roque must complete the verification required by Schedule 1 and provide the complete written verification package specified in clauses 1.6–1.7 **by email to both Homeowners within two calendar weeks (14 calendar days) after the Effective Date**. The Effective Date is excluded from the count; delivery must be completed by 11:59 p.m. Singapore time on the fourteenth calendar day following it, including weekends and public holidays (the **“Verification Deadline”**).

The package must contain the consolidated report and verification record, identified investigators and sources of new material accounts, supporting records and confirmations, Roque's signed corporate adoption after director review, and specific disclosures required by Schedule 1. Historical document authors need not provide fresh reports or signatures. Except for the immediate P.E. requirements in Schedule 1, clause 2.5, a package recording genuine unresolved matters after the required enquiries may satisfy delivery under Schedule 1, clause 1.6, but does not itself authorise proceeding on that basis. It must be provided as readable email text and/or readable attachments sent to the designated email addresses below. Where multiple emails are necessary, they must identify the same package index and version; delivery is complete only when both Homeowners have received all required parts. A download link alone, oral account, incomplete package or promise of later delivery does not satisfy this requirement. Roque must retain the delivery record and remedy any failed delivery within the deadline. A Homeowner's acknowledgement is not required to establish actual receipt and does not certify accuracy.

**Failure to deliver the complete required package by the Verification Deadline constitutes a breach of this Agreement by Roque immediately upon expiry of that deadline, without any reminder, demand or further grace period.** The Agreement remains binding despite that breach. Later delivery does not erase the breach or release accrued rights. Any extension must be expressly agreed in writing by Roque and both Homeowners; silence or continued discussions do not extend the deadline.

Until the complete package has been delivered, Roque must not proceed with regularisation and must ensure that Saege and anyone acting on Roque's behalf do not do so. This restriction includes submitting or authorising a regularisation application, using or submitting a Homeowner's acknowledgement or consent, and commencing physical regularisation works. Investigations, collection of records and enquiries necessary to prepare the verification package are permitted and required. Delivering the package does not authorise use of an unsigned or unapproved homeowner declaration, dispense with acceptance of a proposed exception under Schedule 1, section 5, or excuse an identified deficiency in the required verification.

The Homeowners are not required to sign the HDB acknowledgement or provide assistance to implement regularisation before delivery of the complete package and receipt of the upfront fee required by Schedule 2. Review of the verification package may take place before regularisation proceeds. Roque's verification and delivery duties, and its payment obligations under Schedule 2, are binding from the Effective Date; the payment timetable is not deferred until package delivery. Neither receipt nor review transfers responsibility for verification or accuracy to the Homeowners.

Missing or late verification and premature regularisation are breaches of the relevant obligations, not grounds for treating this Agreement as never having taken effect. Rights and remedies for breach remain available; this provision does not itself impose a cash penalty or automatically terminate the Agreement. Absence of required P.E. confirmation at signing or an adverse P.E. response triggers immediate automatic termination under Schedule 1, clauses 2.5 and 5.13. Administrative delivery of existing endorsements and confirmations is separately due within 7 calendar days after the Effective Date; missing that deadline triggers cessation and either Homeowner’s express termination right, rather than automatic termination. Schedule 2 continues to govern earned fees, refunds and repayments, and the reservation of rights under the Original Contract remains applicable.

## Termination and preservation of accrued rights

Absence of required written P.E. confirmation at signing or a P.E. denial, withdrawal or statement of unsuitability triggers immediate substantive warranty breach and automatic termination under Schedule 1, clauses 2.5 and 5.13. Copies of existing endorsements and confirmations must be delivered to both Homeowners within 7 calendar days after the Effective Date. A failure merely to deliver by that deadline triggers cessation and either Homeowner’s right to terminate by notice, without automatically terminating the Agreement or by itself proving substantive falsity. Either Homeowner or Roque may give the notification specified there; Roque must notify both Homeowners promptly upon learning of the trigger. Termination preserves accrued rights, liabilities and payment obligations, warranties already given, records handover, Schedule 2 accounting and the Original Contract admission and reservation. The Agreement takes effect before any termination caused by a breach existing at signing; it is not void from inception.

Either Homeowner may terminate this regularisation Agreement on the grounds and by the notice procedure in Schedule 1, clauses 5.4–5.12, without Roque's or Saege's consent. That right covers findings reasonably justifying rejection of Roque, Saege, the specified project managers, directors, representatives or verification or oversight leads (including Nicholas and Cayden), or the proposed method, and does not require a warranty breach or prior determination of falsity under Schedule 2. Its effects, surviving obligations and Original Contract reservation are as stated in Schedule 1. Schedule 2 governs accounting for earned and unearned assistance fees following termination.

Either Homeowner may also terminate for material breach of the other immediate warranties under Schedule 1, clause 5.14. That right operates by notice, not automatic termination, and preserves accrued obligations as specified there.

## Governing law and dispute resolution

This Agreement is governed by Singapore law. The parties submit disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Singapore courts, without excluding a Singapore statutory tribunal where it has jurisdiction. This provision does not expand any tribunal's jurisdiction or amend the dispute-resolution arrangements applicable to the Original Contract.

A party raising a dispute must email the other parties with the issues, reasons, reasonably available supporting evidence and relief sought. For a termination challenge by Roque, Schedule 1, clauses 5.9–5.12 exclusively govern the objection, discussion and commencement deadlines and the consequences of non-compliance. For other disputes, the parties must make a reasonable effort to resolve the identified issues in writing within 14 calendar days after receipt of the dispute notice. Mediation may be undertaken by written agreement but is not a prerequisite to proceedings. Neither discussions nor mediation suspend a contractual or statutory deadline unless a valid express written extension applies. Nothing prevents proceedings needed to preserve a right or obtain urgent relief, or excuses compliance with applicable procedural rules or binding orders.

During a termination challenge, the independent interim duties in Schedule 1, clause 5.8 apply. Undisputed payments, refunds and other surviving obligations remain due. Schedule 2 governs its own accounting and false-assurance disputes; the termination-challenge time bar does not apply to independent Schedule 2 disputes. No provision requires a court or tribunal to decide a timely commenced dispute within the contractual period for commencing it.
