# Schedule 1 — Drafting Memo

These notes record the rationale for Schedule 1 and are not operative contractual terms.

## Clause 2.5 — Direct director delivery and P.E. evidence

The Homeowners report that, in previous communications, Hanyong, a representative of Roque, repeatedly said that his earlier statements were wrong or revised those statements, explaining that he was not aware of the underlying facts and was merely passing on information supplied to him.

Clause 2.5 is intended to address that experience. Its requirement that Nicholas personally send the P.E. endorsements and confirmations, expressly acting as Roque's director and for and on behalf of Roque, is a deliberate accountability safeguard. The Homeowners require direct delivery at director level of the actual supporting documents, rather than another relayed account that may later be revised on the basis of the representative's lack of knowledge.

The requirement for authentic written confirmations attributable to the P.E. complements that safeguard: Roque's own summary or a verbal account is not a substitute for the P.E.'s confirmation of authorised use and present suitability.

Accordingly, the named director's personal delivery requirement should not be treated as unnecessary administrative detail when simplifying the agreement. It remains a corporate obligation of Roque and does not, by itself, impose a separate personal warranty or guarantee on Nicholas.
