# Schedule 3 — Refundable Security Deposit

Draft for incorporation into the regularisation agreement. Complete the deposit account particulars before signing. This Schedule concerns security for defined reimbursement obligations, separately from Schedule 2's assistance fee.

## 1. Purpose and deposit

1.1 Roque shall provide a refundable security deposit of **S$3,000 in total** for the staircase railings and balcony awning (the **“Deposit”**). The Deposit secures the obligations in section 3, including where verification appears satisfactory when supplied but a material error is discovered later. It is not a participation payment, assistance fee, agreed damages sum or automatic forfeiture upon breach or termination.

1.2 The Deposit shall be paid after both Homeowners have received the complete Schedule 1 verification package and expressly accepted in writing the verified basis for proceeding, including any corrected or unresolved basis requiring acceptance under Schedule 1, section 5. Roque shall pay within **five business days** after receiving that written acceptance and, in every case, before either Homeowner is required to sign or release the HDB Acknowledgement form, grant authority for regularisation, or undertake implementation assistance. Review and investigation necessary for verification may occur before funding. Schedule 2's separate upfront payment obligations remain applicable.

1.3 Receipt of cleared funds is an additional condition of proceeding, not permission to proceed. Roque must not commence or authorise submissions or physical regularisation, use homeowner consent, or allow Saege or its appointees to do so until the Deposit is funded and all other applicable verification, acceptance and authorisation requirements are satisfied. If this Agreement terminates before funding becomes due, no Deposit need be paid, without extinguishing any accrued payment or reimbursement obligation.

1.4 Acceptance of the verified basis, funding, signing or authorisation does not certify the verification's truth, transfer verification responsibility to the Homeowners, qualify a warranty already given, or release claims. This Schedule does not change Schedule 1's warranty commencement, breach or termination rules. Failure to fund when due is a breach; it does not itself automatically terminate the Agreement. The Homeowners may withhold signing, authorisation and implementation assistance while funding is outstanding.

## 2. Holding and records

2.1 The Homeowners shall hold the Deposit jointly in the designated separate bank account below, apart from the assistance fee and their ordinary spending funds. They shall not spend, pledge or apply it except as permitted by this Schedule. Neither Homeowner may authorise a deduction or transfer without the other's written agreement, except pursuant to a binding order. The account arrangement must permit compliance with these restrictions.

2.2 The Homeowners shall acknowledge cleared receipt within two business days and maintain a ledger of receipts, interest, deductions and refunds. They shall supply the ledger and relevant transaction evidence on a release, deduction or reasonable written request, with unrelated personal information redacted. Interest actually credited on the Deposit follows the principal proportionately; no fixed interest return is promised. Roque bears its transfer charges; ordinary holding costs are borne by the Homeowners unless expressly agreed otherwise.

2.3 An independent stakeholder may replace this holding arrangement only by a writing signed by Roque and both Homeowners specifying the stakeholder, fees, funding, drawdown and release instructions. No stakeholder is bound merely by being named in this Schedule.

## 3. Secured reimbursement obligations

3.1 Roque shall reimburse the Homeowners for reasonable, necessary and documented costs and losses caused by:

- A material inaccuracy, falsehood or omission in a Schedule 1 factual assurance or verification account warranted by Roque, assessed as at the date and scope to which that warranty relates, including materially inaccurate reports of checks, evidence or uncertainty.
- Unauthorised commencement or continuation of regularisation, misuse of homeowner consent, or failure to implement applicable cessation duties by Roque, Saege or persons engaged by either for this arrangement.
- Submission or use in this arrangement of forged, materially altered, materially misleading or technically unsuitable documents, or documents used without the rights or authorisation required by the Agreement, by Roque, Saege or those persons.

Roque undertakes the reimbursement obligation for the specified conduct of Saege and those persons; this does not itself make them parties or impose a separate personal guarantee on any director. Dishonesty need not be proved for a warranted material inaccuracy or other covered failure. An expressly unresolved proposition is not treated as warranted true merely because the Homeowners accepted proceeding with uncertainty.

3.2 Qualifying costs may include obtaining and authenticating submission records; reasonable independent P.E. inspection, measurements, testing and document review; correcting submissions and obtaining necessary replacement drawings, calculations, endorsements or approvals; and necessary fresh regularisation, safety measures or physical rectification attributable to a covered event. Reasonable legal advice or correspondence necessary to investigate or correct the covered event may qualify. Costs of court or tribunal proceedings remain subject to applicable law and the competent forum's costs orders, without duplicate recovery under this Schedule.

3.3 Ordinary regularisation expenses already payable by Roque remain its responsibility and cannot be shifted to the Deposit. Unrelated renovation defects, unrelated Original Contract claims, minor unrelated errors, later changes or new regulatory requirements without a covered failure, and unsupported distress or inconvenience claims are outside this security. A future HDB enquiry or revised position alone does not prove an earlier warranty false. Additional homeowner time is payable only under an expressly agreed fee arrangement or an otherwise established legal entitlement; Schedule 2's reference rates do not automatically price it here.

3.4 The Homeowners shall take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary costs and provide invoices, receipts or other reasonable evidence of incurred loss. Where practicable, they shall notify Roque before material corrective expenditure and allow a reasonable written response, without requiring its permission, delaying urgent safety action or compliance with an authority's deadline, or accepting Roque or Saege as the corrective contractor. Roque may suggest reasonable alternatives; the Homeowners shall reasonably consider them.

3.5 Reimbursement is not capped by the Deposit. There shall be no double recovery from the Deposit, Roque, Saege, insurance or another source. A covered event can create a reimbursement obligation despite HDB approval, but neither termination nor approval alone establishes that obligation or its amount.

## 4. Claim notice, reservation and deductions

4.1 Either Homeowner may notify a security claim by email to Roque, copied to the other Homeowner, before the applicable Release Date. The notice shall identify the affected assurance or conduct, reasonably available evidence, causal connection to corrective costs or loss, amounts incurred or reasonably estimated, and the sum proposed to be reserved. An itemised professional quotation may support an estimate. Explain unavailable evidence and further enquiries. A general allegation or mere suspicion without supporting facts does not justify retention.

4.2 A timely, evidence-supported notice permits reservation of only the amount reasonably required for that claim, up to the remaining Deposit. Reservation preserves security pending determination; it is not a finding of liability or authority to spend the reserved funds. Roque may dispute the event, scope, estimate or retention by reasoned written response with available evidence. The main agreement's dispute process applies; no silence or failure to respond is deemed an admission.

4.3 A deduction may be made only for an incurred, qualifying amount established by Roque's written agreement, a written settlement signed by Roque and both Homeowners, or a binding court or tribunal determination. Agreed or determined amounts are payable by Roque within 14 calendar days; the Homeowners may apply the corresponding Deposit amount with an itemised accounting, crediting it against that obligation. Roque may pay directly instead, in which case the corresponding reserved sum becomes releasable subject to other claims. No automatic forfeiture, daily fine or deduction for estimated future expenditure applies.

4.4 The Homeowners shall review reservations at least every 30 calendar days, provide a short update and revised support, and release excess amounts within 14 calendar days of identifying them. If Roque disputes a reservation, the parties shall attempt written resolution under the main agreement. To continue holding a disputed reservation, the Homeowners must commence proceedings seeking determination of the covered claim or entitlement to retain security within **60 calendar days after receipt of Roque's reasoned dispute**, unless the parties expressly agree another period or a binding order applies. Otherwise that disputed reservation shall be refunded within 14 calendar days. This security-release consequence does not extinguish the underlying claim or extend a statutory limitation period. Timely commenced proceedings allow the reasonable reservation to remain until settlement or determination, subject to review and binding orders.

## 5. Completion and termination closeout

5.1 The **“Completion Closeout Date”** is the later of:

- Receipt by both Homeowners of written HDB confirmation identifying both Specified Works as regularised, with all conditions necessary for completion satisfied; and
- Receipt by both Homeowners of the complete final record: applications and subsequent corrections, submitted documents and versions, declarations and homeowner authorisations actually used, P.E. endorsements and relevant confirmations, HDB correspondence, approvals, completion evidence, and a consolidated index identifying any outstanding matter.

The record must accurately identify unavailable items and efforts to obtain them. HDB approval alone or a contractor's assurance alone does not start the period. The Homeowners' receipt or review is not certification of accuracy or safety.

5.2 If the Agreement terminates, the **“Termination Closeout Date”** is the date both Homeowners receive a complete, evidence-supported closeout account showing:

- Delivery of the submission history and records required by Schedule 1, including any steps taken after termination and pending applications or scheduled actions.
- Stop instructions delivered to Saege, relevant submitters, P.E.s, installers and other appointees, and their responses or evidence of implementation; accurately recorded unsuccessful contact attempts must not be presented as confirmation.
- Cessation of use of homeowner consents and accurate written notification to HDB of the ended authority where an application, approval or active submission makes that notification relevant, with delivery evidence and any response received.
- Identification and appropriate handling of pending actions and discovered unauthorised submissions, including corrections communicated to HDB and written clarification from HDB where approval status or further required action remains in question.

Termination does not itself cancel HDB approval or authorise its withdrawal. Legally required or immediate safety action remains governed by Schedule 1. A specific unresolved covered exposure is handled by reservation under section 4 rather than withholding unrelated funds indefinitely.

5.3 Roque may propose either closeout date by email with an indexed package. Within 14 calendar days, the Homeowners shall identify any material deficiency and its relevance. A minor unrelated omission does not prevent closeout. Silence is not acceptance, but the date is determined objectively by fulfilment of these requirements, not unfettered homeowner discretion. A dispute over the date or missing records is governed by the main agreement; either side may seek a determination. Completion of routine HDB correspondence is not required where there is no unresolved status issue and Roque has supplied evidence of the necessary notification.

## 6. Release and survival

6.1 The **“Release Date”** is six calendar months after the Completion Closeout Date, or, if the Agreement terminates before release, six calendar months after the Termination Closeout Date. If the corresponding day does not exist in the sixth month, use its last day. Termination is not itself a ground for forfeiture. An accounting of the Deposit is separate from Schedule 2's accounting.

6.2 Within 14 calendar days after the Release Date, the Homeowners shall return the Deposit and attributable credited interest, less deductions permitted under clause 4.3 and reasonable reservations for qualifying claims notified by that date. They shall simultaneously supply an itemised accounting. A claim notified in time may be resolved afterwards. A claim notified after release does not revive an obligation to fund this Deposit, but underlying rights remain unaffected.

6.3 Following settlement, final binding determination or cessation of a reservation under clause 4.4, the releasable reserved balance and attributable interest shall be refunded within 14 calendar days. No unrelated claim may delay an undisputed refund. Deposit funding, reservation, deduction and release do not settle Original Contract claims, certify verification, waive warranties or revive terminated authority. Any release of claims requires an express separate written agreement.

6.4 The holding, reimbursement, accounting, claim, dispute and release obligations survive completion and termination. Schedule 1 governs termination and interim cessation duties; Schedule 2 exclusively governs the assistance fee; this Schedule governs the Deposit and its defined reimbursement obligations. The main agreement's governing law and dispute provisions apply. Schedule 1's termination-challenge deadlines and Schedule 2's false-assurance establishment procedure do not govern independent security claims under this Schedule.

6.5 Notices use the designated email addresses in the main agreement. A business day excludes Saturdays, Sundays and Singapore public holidays. Exclude the triggering date when counting days; calendar-day periods include weekends and public holidays and expire at 11:59 p.m. Singapore time. Mandatory law and binding orders prevail. Changes require a writing signed by Roque and both Homeowners.
