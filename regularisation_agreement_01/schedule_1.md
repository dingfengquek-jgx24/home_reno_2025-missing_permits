# Schedule 1 — Material Assurances Concerning Roque and Saege

This Schedule forms part of the Agreement. “Specified Works” means the staircase railings and balcony awning identified in the Agreement. Roque gives the immediate warranties below from the Effective Date. Other historical and factual assurances are proposals for investigation and adoption or correction by Roque; they become warranties only on the Verification Date. This Schedule records proposed contractual terms, not findings by the Homeowners.

## 1. Verification and warranties

**1.1 Reliance.** The Homeowners rely on the verified information when deciding whether to continue this arrangement, engage Roque, accept its engagement of Saege for the Specified Works, and accept the identified managers, directors, representatives and verification or oversight leads, including Nicholas and Cayden in their verified capacities. Honesty, reliability, suitability and the proposed regularisation basis are material to those decisions. A matter is material if it could reasonably affect them.

**1.2 Deadline and single process.** By **[agreed calendar date], 11:59 p.m. Singapore time** (the **Verification Deadline**), Roque must give both Homeowners one complete, supported written verification package. It must cover every assurance and enquiry in this Schedule, including the immediate signing matters, both P.E. documents, each company's and relevant persons' conduct, disclosures and any updates. Any extension must be agreed expressly in writing by Roque and both Homeowners. Missing the deadline is a breach; it does not invalidate the Agreement or defer payment obligations.

**1.3 Verification Date and warranties.** The **Verification Date** is when both Homeowners have actually received every required part of the package, including Nicholas's P.E. delivery under clause 2.5 and Roque's signed corporate adoption under clause 1.5; if receipt dates differ, the later date applies. On that date, Roque warrants that the factual assurances in sections 2–4, as specifically adopted or corrected in the package, are true and accurate in all material respects and not misleading by material omission. Current facts are warranted as at that date; historical facts relate to the periods identified. Roque also warrants that the reported enquiries and review occurred and that the package is materially accurate. An expressly unresolved proposition is not warranted as established, but the account of enquiries, evidence and uncertainty must be accurate and complete. These warranties are not retrospective.

**1.4 Register and evidence.** The package must include one indexed register with an entry for each assurance and material question. Entries must state the precise adopted, corrected or unresolved outcome; relevant persons, capacities, works and dates; enquiries and sources; supporting and contradictory records; limitations, follow-up and review dates; and any disclosure and express homeowner acceptance. Separate entries where needed to distinguish persons, companies, works or dates. Identify investigators and sources of new accounts, their roles and knowledge limits. Obtain written confirmation of new material factual accounts where available; accurately record unconfirmed oral accounts and unavailable records. Existing records may be indexed without duplication, and historical document authors need not prepare new reports. Do not claim checks or record review that did not occur. The signing-stage record and P.E. materials must retain their original dates.

**1.5 Director review and adoption.** A Roque director must oversee and review every register entry and supporting record, make further enquiries where accounts conflict or are incomplete, and assess source limitations. The director must sign for and on behalf of Roque after review:

> Roque confirms that the enquiries and director's review required by this Schedule have been completed and are accurately described in the identified Register and Package. Roque adopts the factual assurances as specifically stated or corrected there, with unresolved matters expressly identified. Roque confirms that the package accurately reports material evidence, inconsistencies, limitations and outstanding matters.

This is Roque's corporate adoption and creates no separate personal warranty or guarantee by the director.

**1.6 Immediate warranties at signing.** From the Effective Date, Roque warrants that: (a) its legal identity and UEN are correct and its signatory is authorised to bind it; (b) Saege's stated identity is correct and it holds the registrations and authorisations required for its proposed role, with no restriction preventing that role; (c) Saege has agreed in writing to its regularisation role and verification cooperation; (d) the recorded current personnel, responsibilities and authority are accurate and agreed; (e) signing documents supplied by or for Roque are authentic copies, accurately described and without undisclosed material alteration or omission; and (f) Roque has consulted relevant representatives and reviewed available relevant records, and has disclosed known material contrary information, notices, restrictions and deadlines. Any extract, redaction, limitation or expressly agreed exception must be identified in the signing record; an exception is effective only if expressly agreed in writing by Roque and both Homeowners. No exception applies to section 2. These warranties concern present facts and enquiries, not unverified historical responsibility.

## 4. Disclosure, acceptance and termination

**4.1 No proceeding before verification and acceptance.** Roque must not submit or authorise a regularisation application, use homeowner consents, or permit physical regularisation until both Homeowners have received the complete package and expressly accepted in writing the basis for proceeding, including any corrected or unresolved matter or changed personnel/method. Silence, receipt, review, payment or participation is not acceptance or release. Acceptance cannot waive section 2 requirements or authorise excluded work under clause 2.2. The Homeowners may rely on Roque's adopted warranties without independently investigating them.

**4.2 Continuing disclosure and supplements.** From the Effective Date until completion or termination, Roque must promptly disclose to both Homeowners any material discovery, change, error, inconsistent account or resolution affecting this Schedule, and in any event before the next affected submission or step. If discovered after submission, Roque must promptly correct the submission with its recipient and copy both Homeowners. Materially changed arrangements require express written acceptance before proceeding. Updated factual warranties arise only after verification, director review, signed corporate adoption and delivery of a supplement to both Homeowners; identify affected entries, evidence and dates and preserve prior versions. Nicholas must personally deliver any new or revised P.E. records as required by clause 2.1. An update does not retrospectively qualify an existing warranty or release an accrued claim.

**4.3 Termination for resolved findings.** Either Homeowner may terminate this regularisation Agreement by written notice to Roque, copying the other Homeowner, if a matter previously unresolved is resolved or sufficiently clarified by evidence and gives reasonable grounds not to continue with Roque, Saege, specified personnel (including Nicholas or Cayden in their verified capacities) or the proposed method. The notice must identify the matter, resulting facts, available evidence and reasons. Mere suspicion or continuing uncertainty alone is insufficient for this termination right, but uncertainty prevents proceeding absent the written acceptance required by clause 4.1. Termination takes effect on Roque's receipt or a later date stated in the notice; Roque's consent or a prior court/tribunal decision is not required.

**4.4 P.E. triggers and delivery failure.** The Agreement automatically terminates if (a) a required written P.E. confirmation is not obtained by the Verification Deadline, or (b) a P.E. denies or withdraws the required authorisation or states that a document or installation is unsuitable. For an existing denial or withdrawal, termination occurs when the Agreement takes effect; for a later statement, when communicated to Roque, Saege, their representative or the Homeowners. A later confirmation does not revive the Agreement; a new arrangement requires a fresh written agreement signed by all parties.

If the confirmations were obtained by the deadline but Roque fails to deliver the complete package, including Nicholas's required personal delivery, by that deadline, it is a breach but not automatic termination or proof of substantive falsity. Roque and its appointees must cease regularisation and use of homeowner consents. Either Homeowner may terminate by notice identifying the delivery failure. Later delivery does not erase the breach or accrued termination right; resumption requires a fresh written agreement signed by all parties.

**4.5 Other immediate-warranty breach and effects.** Either Homeowner may terminate by written notice for a material breach of clause 1.6. Minor unrelated errors do not trigger this right. For any termination or challenge, Roque must stop regularisation and use of homeowner consents, ensure its appointees do likewise, and promptly hand over submission status and records (within five business days after a notice invoking clauses 4.3 or 4.5, or a clause 4.4 delivery breach). Necessary action required by law or immediately needed for safety is permitted, with prompt notice to both Homeowners. No termination charge is payable merely for exercising a valid right. Accrued rights, warranties already given, payment obligations, record handover, evidence preservation, Schedule 2 accounting and the Original Contract reservation survive. Schedule 2 governs the fee and refund for unperformed assistance; termination alone does not prove a warranty false.

Roque must email any challenge to a homeowner termination to both Homeowners within 14 calendar days, with reasons, available evidence and remedy sought. It must commence proceedings in a competent Singapore court or tribunal and email proof of filing within 60 calendar days after receiving the notice. Discussions do not suspend these deadlines. Interim cessation, handover and payment duties continue during a challenge. Calendar periods exclude the triggering date and expire at 11:59 p.m. Singapore time; a business day excludes Singapore weekends and public holidays. Mandatory law and binding orders prevail.
