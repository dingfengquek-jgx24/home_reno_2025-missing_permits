Draft for incorporation into the regularisation agreement. The statements below are proposed assurances for verification and adoption or correction by Roque; they are not independent findings by the Homeowners. Signing creates the investigation, disclosure and other contractual obligations, but does not give the factual warranties in this Schedule before verification. Complete signing particulars before execution and verification particulars at the stage specified below.

## 1. Reliance, verification and commencement of warranties

Honesty, reliability, suitability and the validity of the proposed regularisation are material independently of whether regularisation succeeds. A fact, inaccuracy or omission is material if it would reasonably affect any of those decisions; a minor unrelated error is not material.

1.2 The Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date. Roque must complete the enquiries and review required below and email the complete verification package to both Homeowners by the Verification Deadline defined in the main agreement. Missing that deadline is a breach without making the Agreement ineffective or postponing payment obligations. The package may record genuinely unresolved matters after the required enquiries, as specified in clauses 1.6 and 5.2; it may not omit those enquiries or present uncertainty as a verified conclusion.

1.3 The **“Verification Date”** is the date on which, after the required enquiries and director's review have been completed, both Homeowners have received the complete package and Roque's signed corporate adoption under clause 1.7. If receipt occurs on different dates, the later date applies. The Verification Date must occur by the Verification Deadline; late completion does not erase the delivery breach. A director's earlier signature alone does not start the warranties. If Roque delivers a package and corporate adoption representing that the required verification is complete, it cannot avoid the warranties attaching to that delivered account by later asserting that its represented checks or review did not actually occur; a false claim of completion remains subject to clause 1.4.

1.4 Only on the Verification Date does Roque represent and warrant that the factual assurances in sections 2–4, as specifically adopted or corrected in the package, are true and accurate in all material respects and not misleading through material omission. Current facts are warranted as at that date; historical assurances concern the expressly identified historical periods. The warranties are given after verification, not retrospectively at the Effective Date. A proposed proposition expressly identified as unresolved is not warranted as established; the accuracy and completeness of the account of enquiries, evidence and uncertainty are nevertheless warranted. Roque also warrants that the reported checks and review actually occurred and that the factual contents of the package and corporate adoption are materially accurate and not misleading. These are material assurances for section 6. Performing checks does not excuse a false adopted conclusion or reduce an adopted factual warranty to knowledge or belief.

1.5 The director signing for Roque must oversee and review verification of every proposed assurance in sections 2–4. Assistance from persons with relevant knowledge, competence and records is permitted. The director must assess sources and limitations, require further enquiries where accounts are incomplete or inconsistent, and address the actual decision-makers and directly involved persons. Lack of knowledge, recollection, involvement or a PM title is not evidence that conduct did not occur. Unsuccessful enquiries must be reported accurately without suggesting that the underlying matter was resolved.

## 4. Conduct of Roque and Saege, including persons who were not PMs

The following proposed assurances must be investigated separately for Roque and Saege and adopted or corrected under section 1. Roque gives the adopted warranties concerning both companies; describing Saege's conduct does not itself make Saege a party to this Agreement. Identify and investigate the relevant persons acting for each company, including directors, employees and representatives who were not PMs. The enquiries must expressly address Nicholas and Cayden in their respective verified capacities, as well as Hanyong, under clause 3.7.

4.1 Neither company, through a PM or a person who was not a PM, instructed, authorised or agreed that the Specified Works proceed when a required prior HDB permit had not been obtained. Verify expressly that no non-PM acting for Roque did so and, separately, that no non-PM acting for Saege did so. Report the checks actually made before each authorisation, who checked, the evidence checked and what was communicated; do not infer that checking occurred because it should have occurred. Identify any authorisation given without checking whether the required permits had been obtained, and any authorisation despite knowledge that they had not. An unsupported assumption that another person had dealt with permits must be disclosed.

4.2 Neither company knowingly decided, directed or agreed to proceed in intentional disregard of applicable statutory permit obligations, including by intentionally withholding permit instructions. Investigate the relevant persons' knowledge, communications, decisions and actual conduct. Distinguish verified facts from any disputed legal characterisation; a final regulatory or court finding is not required merely to disclose adverse facts or exercise clause 5.4 on its stated basis.

4.3 Neither company deliberately withheld from the Homeowners its knowledge of missing permit applications or approvals, an authorisation without checking, or a decision or arrangement described in clauses 4.1–4.2. Identify any relevant information withheld between the companies as part of the verified account.

4.4 Neither company knowingly represented to the Homeowners that a permit application or approval had been completed when it had not. Identify the relevant statements, dates, speakers and company capacities; disclose material misleading accounts even where deliberate conduct cannot be established.

4.5 Neither company charged for or collected payment for permit-related services while having decided those services would not be performed. Identify separately the relevant services, payer, payee, charges, payments and decisions, including relevant inter-company arrangements. If there were no relevant Saege charges or payments, state that verified fact rather than assume Saege charged the Homeowners directly. 4.6 An administrative omission, authorisation without checking, knowing authorisation of unpermitted works, concealment, false representation and misleading charging are distinct issues. Report findings and unresolved matters for each rather than treating the proposed account in clause 3.1 as resolving them all. Their materiality does not depend on regularisation failing.

5.2 Known issues must be recorded at signing, but their recording is not a factual warranty before verification or an acceptance unless expressly stated. The complete package may accurately document a matter that remains unresolved after the required enquiries. That can satisfy the package-delivery obligation, but Roque must not proceed on any corrected, qualified or unresolved basis unless both Homeowners expressly accept that basis in writing. Silence, receipt, review, payment and continued discussions are not acceptance. Unaffected adopted assurances remain warranted. Acceptance of proceeding despite uncertainty is not acceptance in advance of whatever adverse facts may later emerge.

5.3 An updated account or resolution must be disclosed under clause 1.9. Before warranties commence, correction of a proposed assurance is not itself a breach of that proposed warranty. After a warranty is given, later disclosure or acceptance does not retrospectively qualify it or release an accrued claim. Acceptance of a fact or permission to proceed does not by itself release original-renovation claims; an intended release requires a separate express written agreement.

5.4 **Either Homeowner may unilaterally terminate this regularisation Agreement by written notice to Roque if a matter previously unresolved is resolved, or sufficiently clarified by evidence, and the resulting facts provide reasonable grounds for that Homeowner not to agree to or continue on the terms of this arrangement.** This includes reasonable grounds not to engage Roque, accept Roque's engagement of Saege as subcontractor, accept a specified project manager, director, representative or verification or oversight lead (expressly including Nicholas and Cayden in their verified capacities), or accept the proposed regularisation method. It applies to matters unresolved at signing or arising later, whether or not previously entered in the disclosure table. Examples include authorisation of unpermitted Specified Works by a PM or non-PM without checking permit status, intentional disregard of statutory permit obligations, a materially misleading account of involvement, or material ineligibility for the proposed role. Mere suspicion or continuing uncertainty alone is not a resolution for this clause; section 5.2 continues to govern permission to proceed while a matter remains unresolved.

5.5 The notice must identify the matter, resulting facts, reasonably available supporting evidence and why those facts justify not continuing, and must be emailed to Roque's designated address with a copy to the other Homeowner. Termination takes effect on Roque's receipt or a later date stated in the notice. It does not require Roque's or Saege's consent, a prior court or tribunal determination, proof of a warranty breach, failure of regularisation or acceptance of a replacement contractor, manager, director, representative or verification or oversight lead. The establishment requirements in Schedule 2 for a false assurance govern fee treatment only and are not prerequisites to this termination right. A dispute about whether the clause's requirements were met remains subject to the applicable dispute process; the notice does not require advance determination through that process.

5.6 On termination Roque must stop further regularisation works and submissions under this Agreement, ensure that Saege and its other appointees do likewise, cease further use of homeowner consents for this arrangement, and promptly provide both Homeowners with the records and status of submissions already made. Any necessary notification to HDB must accurately report the cessation without making a fresh application or withdrawing an existing approval without the Homeowners' written authority. This does not prevent action required by law or immediately necessary to address a safety risk; Roque must notify both Homeowners promptly of such action. No termination charge or damages for the mere exercise of a valid clause 5.4 right are payable by the Homeowners. Accrued rights, warranties already given, record-handover obligations, Schedule 2 accounting and the Original Contract reservation survive; future performance obligations end to the extent not expressly preserved.

5.7 This right terminates the regularisation Agreement, not the Original Contract, and does not release any original-renovation claim. Schedule 2 governs earned fees, refunds of unearned advances and any repayment; termination neither automatically forfeits the fee nor proves an assurance false. Other available termination rights are preserved.

## 6. Relationship with the assistance fee

6.1 The factual warranties given after verification under clauses 1.4 and 1.9, including the warranted factual contents and adoption of the package or a supplement, are the material assurances for Schedule 2's second completion condition. An unresolved proposition is not itself a false warranty; a materially false or misleading account of its status, enquiries or evidence may be. Successful regularisation does not establish truth, and later regulatory action does not itself establish historical falsity.

6.2 Schedule 2 exclusively governs earning, refund, repayment and later recovery of the assistance fee. Work performed earns the fee; false assurances do not earn unperformed fees. No additional cash penalty or release is created here. Termination and fee treatment remain distinct as specified in section 5.
