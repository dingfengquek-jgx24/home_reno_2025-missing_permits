# Drafting Notes — Not Part of the Agreement

The agreement and its schedules remain drafts. These notes are not operative contractual terms.

- Complete Schedule 1's document references, assurances, disclosures and unresolved factual statements before signing. Do not replace specific facts with broad references to reputation or a guarantee that approval can never be overturned.
- Complete the parties' legal details, property and works description, agreement date and signatures in the main agreement. Identify the final versions of both schedules in the signed package.
- Agree Schedule 2's upfront payment period, the precise regularisation completion documents, and the main agreement's dispute-resolution process. Define when a stalled arrangement may be terminated so unperformed work does not remain pending indefinitely.
- Schedule 2 sets a six-calendar-month notice period after repayment for later-discovered false assurances. A notice within that period may be resolved after it expires.
- The earlier S$600 and S$75 cash-penalty provisions have been replaced by the Schedule 2 assistance-fee arrangements. The main agreement's homeowner-assistance scope now refers to Schedule 2 rather than stating that signing a form is the only assistance required.
- Obtain Singapore legal review of the assurance-linked repayment and later-discovery provisions. Describing them as repayment conditions does not itself resolve whether they operate as breach remedies subject to the penalty rule. The draft does not assert guaranteed enforceability.
